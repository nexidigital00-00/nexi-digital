Common questions

You do this with AI? Then is it real expertise?

Real expertise. Opening the tool is the easy part. Knowing which work to hand to AI, and how to set it up so the team actually uses it, takes experience. I'm not just claiming it; my work shows it.

My team isn't technical. Will it still work?

Yes. That's why training is part of setup. I build screens that work with one tap, and I stay until the team is comfortable.

Do you work outside Sakarya?

Yes. On-site in and around Sakarya, online anywhere in Turkey. A Discovery Day can be done online too.

What happens to my customer data?

Data is used only for your business, in your own accounts, and is never shared with third parties. We prepare KVKK-compliant notices and consent steps together during setup.

Which tools do you use?

It depends on the job: AI models like Claude and ChatGPT, WhatsApp Business, Google tools and, when needed, small web apps built for you. Licences and accounts are opened in your name.

When does the monthly fee end?

Whenever you like. Monthly Support runs month to month, with no commitment. The systems stay with you when you leave.

What if I don't continue after the Discovery Day?

That's fine. The automation set up that day and the 30-day roadmap stay with you; you can carry them out with your own team.
